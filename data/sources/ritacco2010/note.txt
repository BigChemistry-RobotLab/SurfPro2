The temperatures in table 1 are reported as 283 and 298 K, however, in the main text their are reported as 283.15 and 298.15 K.
The latter values have been reported here.

Also note the following conditions: 'All aqueous trisiloxane solutions were made in a phosphate buffer (pH 7) in order to avoid hydrolysis'.

The compounds are decribed as 'trisiloxanes', which formally is too generic to assign a specific structure.
However, the term has been interpreted here as referring to compounds with the following SMILES headgroup: 'C[Si](C)(O[Si](O[Si](C)(C)C)(C)CCCO)C'.
This would be consistent with similar structures cited by the paper in 10.1016/S0021-9797(03)00530-7 and 10.1021/la980072s.
